Landing-page media. Photos are Unsplash stock (Unsplash License: free commercial use, no
attribution required; credited here for provenance). The hero recording is our own screen capture.
Re-fetch a photo crop with ?w=<width>&q=85&fm=jpg on the `photo-…` URL. Photos are committed as
grayscale 1600/800-wide or 240px-square crops; the video is re-encoded, not the raw capture.

Subject rule: one person at a screen, or an empty desk. Never a candidate facing an interviewer.
Those photos read as a human-led interview, which is the opposite of what this product is.

demo-hero.mp4                                 screen recording of a practice run, 8x speed
    1280x668, 30fps, no audio, 1.2MB. Built from a 1900x992/60fps capture:
    ffmpeg -i capture-8x.mp4 -an -vf "scale=1280:-2:flags=lanczos,fps=30" \
      -c:v libx264 -preset slow -crf 32 -pix_fmt yuv420p -profile:v high -g 48 \
      -movflags +faststart demo-hero.mp4
    Raw captures are 25-55MB and are gitignored (frontend/public/maxterview-demo-vid-*.mp4).
