Landing-page media. The hero recording is a screen capture of this product, re-encoded rather than
shipped raw. Nothing references it since the hero card became a vector separator (Landing.tsx); the
files stay so the recording can come back without a re-encode. Every photograph that used to sit
beside it was Unsplash stock and has been removed: the page is type, hairlines, a dot grid and the
product's own output now, so there is nothing third-party left to license or attribute.

demo-hero.mp4                                 screen recording of a practice run, 8x speed
    1280x668, 30fps, no audio, 1.2MB. Built from a 1900x992/60fps capture:
    ffmpeg -i capture-8x.mp4 -an -vf "scale=1280:-2:flags=lanczos,fps=30" \
      -c:v libx264 -preset slow -crf 32 -pix_fmt yuv420p -profile:v high -g 48 \
      -movflags +faststart demo-hero.mp4
    Raw captures are 25-55MB and are gitignored (frontend/public/maxterview-demo-vid-*.mp4).

symbols.woff2                                 the grade marks, U+25A4/25CB/2605/2713/2715/2717
    1.3KB subset of Noto Sans Symbols 2 (SIL OFL 1.1, github.com/google/fonts/ofl/notosanssymbols2).
    None of the three text families carries these codepoints, so without it every mark (the lexicon,
    the class tiles, the turn log) is drawn by whatever symbol font the OS happens to ship and the
    page looks different on each platform. Rebuild:
    curl -sSLO https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/notosanssymbols2/NotoSansSymbols2-Regular.ttf
    pyftsubset NotoSansSymbols2-Regular.ttf \
      --unicodes=U+0394,U+2192,U+22EE,U+258B,U+25A4,U+25CB,U+2605,U+2713,U+2715,U+2717 \
      --flavor=woff2 --layout-features='' --no-hinting --desubroutinize --output-file=symbols.woff2
    Only those six landed: the source face has no U+0394/2192/22EE/258B, so nothing is lost by listing
    them. U+0394 is Greek and lives in a Noto Sans subset; U+2192 in Noto Sans Symbols (v1); U+22EE
    and U+258B in neither. Those three fall back on the app pages that use them (none on the landing).
