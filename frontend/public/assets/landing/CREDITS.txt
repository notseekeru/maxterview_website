Landing-page media. There is exactly one asset here and it is ours: the hero recording is a screen
capture of this product, re-encoded rather than shipped raw. Every photograph that used to sit beside
it was Unsplash stock and has been removed: the page is type, hairlines, a dot grid and the product's
own output now, so there is nothing third-party left to license or attribute.

demo-hero.mp4                                 screen recording of a practice run, 8x speed
    1280x668, 30fps, no audio, 1.2MB. Built from a 1900x992/60fps capture:
    ffmpeg -i capture-8x.mp4 -an -vf "scale=1280:-2:flags=lanczos,fps=30" \
      -c:v libx264 -preset slow -crf 32 -pix_fmt yuv420p -profile:v high -g 48 \
      -movflags +faststart demo-hero.mp4
    Raw captures are 25-55MB and are gitignored (frontend/public/maxterview-demo-vid-*.mp4).
